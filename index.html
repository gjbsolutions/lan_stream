Each command is written as one line, terminated with \n: UP:PRESS, UP:RELEASE, DOWN:PRESS, LEFT:RELEASE, RIGHT:PRESS, TOGGLE:ON, TOGGLE:OFF.
If the viewer disconnects or goes silent for 8 s, all held arrows get RELEASE and the toggle gets TOGGLE:OFF.
On MicroPython, read lines with sys.stdin.readline() and split on ':'.
